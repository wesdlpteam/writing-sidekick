## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM assessment: not template slop — the exercise-book system is a bespoke, coherent choice. Two tells: Duolingo-style 3D push buttons (gamified visual grammar despite "not video-gamey" brief) and one-size-fits-6-to-12 juvenile styling (year level is asked first, then never influences the UI).

Deterministic scan: CLI clean (0 findings on index.html + css/app.css). Browser overlay found 3: (1) heading low-contrast 1.8:1 — FALSE POSITIVE, detector resolves transparent backgrounds as black; real contrast is ~10.8:1; (2) disabled "Let's go" button 1.7:1 — measured true, though WCAG exempts disabled controls; (3) cream page background — taste flag, moot since direction is moving to "bright, energetic, fun".

## Overall Impression

A charming, honest, well-structured app whose look no longer matches its owner's target feel ("bright, energetic, fun") and whose single biggest design idea (year-level awareness) is collected but never used visually. Strong bones, wrong energy, one real safety gap (one-tap destruction of a child's work).

## What's Working

- Review screen IA: scan thumbnail beside editable transcript = recognition over recall.
- Error/correction copy: blame-free, actionable, jargon-free ("Fix any words the app got wrong, then keep going").
- Privacy claim matches the code: no storage anywhere, `no-store` on the API; the promise is real.

## Priority Issues

- [P0] Year level never adapts the UI. Emoji headings, bubble font, cursive taped notes shown identically to Year 1 and Year 6; contradicts "Year 6 must not feel talked down to". Fix: two visual tiers keyed off yearLevel (Years 5-6: no heading emoji, cleaner wish typography, flattened cards). Suggested: /impeccable bolder + implementation.
- [P1] "Start again" is one-tap destructive, no confirm/undo, adjacent to "Save or print". Fix: confirm step + visual separation. Suggested: /impeccable harden.
- [P1] The designated delight moment (feedback reveal) is a sub-second fade — quieter than the loading screen before it. Fix: give the reveal a real beat (longer stagger, hold before wish; respect reduced-motion). Suggested: /impeccable animate / delight.
- [P2] Accessibility: gold focus ring ~1.8:1 (needs 3:1); coral titles ~4.0-4.25:1; no aria-live/role on #loading or #error-banner (waiting state invisible to screen readers). Fix: darken ring + coral one step; role="status" aria-live="polite" on loading, role="alert" on error. Suggested: /impeccable audit fixes.
- [P3] Review screen is a navigational dead end (no back); Year (6) and Genre (6) selectors exceed the ≤4 guideline (mitigated by grid chunking and "Not sure" default).

## Persona Red Flags

- Jordan (Year 1 first-timer): raw textarea proofreading assumes fluency a 6yo lacks; "Persuasive"/"Information report" are curriculum vocabulary with no icons; start screen leans entirely on reading.
- Sam (accessibility): focus ring 1.8:1; loading/error states unannounced to screen readers; positives — native dialog focus trap, reduced-motion respected, 44px targets.
- Maya (Year 6): every heading has an emoji, bubble font everywhere, cursive sticky-note feedback with wand emoji, literal star sticker on selection — nothing shifts for her. Most at-odds persona.

## Minor Observations

- Two-phase loading copy ("Tidying up your photo…" → "Reading your writing…") is genuinely good.
- Disabled start button contrast is debatable (WCAG-exempt) but easy to improve.
- Buttons' 3D push grammar borrows from gamified apps; consider whether the new direction keeps it.

## Questions to Consider

- If year level is the first question, why doesn't the answer shape typography, tone, or emoji density downstream?
- Is a 0.25s fade really the payoff the brief calls "the peak moment"?
- Would a one-tap no-undo "delete everything" button survive review in any adult product?
