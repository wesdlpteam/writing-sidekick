The source supports ambitious sentence work and manageable scaffolds together. We retain Nathan's requirement that models use a different, familiar topic from the student's quoted passage, so the student must apply the strategy themselves. NAPLAN remains a guide to writing quality; TWR specifies how to teach the chosen change. Neither source replaces year-level expectations.

The app does not add a full scrambled-sentence, note-taking or classroom assessment course. This integration improves feedback and model correctness within the existing three-step power-up flow. The instructional levels in the book are not mapped directly to Australian year numbers.

Nathan's subsequent correction: persuasive topic sentences must remain short and concise. Evidence and elaboration follow in supporting sentences. The initial integration applied sentence expansion too broadly; preserving the kernel alone did not ensure the strategy suited that sentence's role. Generation and review now explicitly select elaboration for an underdeveloped paragraph with an effective topic sentence, retaining that sentence and adding support after it. This classroom requirement is distinguished from the Section I source summary rather than attributed to the chapter as a universal sentence-length rule.
