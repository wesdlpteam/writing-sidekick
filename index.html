      ✍️ Pick up your pencil

      Go back to your book and change your own writing. Here is how.

      1. To add words, write a ^ where they go in. Write the new words above it.
      2. To swap a word, draw one neat line through it. Write the new word above.
      3. For a new sentence, use the space at the end of a line, or the next line.
      4. You do not need to copy it all out again. Just read it again to check.
